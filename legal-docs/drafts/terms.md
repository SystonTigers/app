## Part A: Using Boost Huddle

### A1. What Boost Huddle is

Boost Huddle is an app and website that clubs use to run their teams: fixtures and results, squads, availability, news, photos and videos, Man of the Match votes, events and club payments. Your club decides which features it uses and who can join.

### A2. Accounts

- You need to be 13 or over to have your own account. Children under 13 should use the app through a parent's or carer's account.
- If you are under 18, please check with a parent or carer before signing up; they should read these terms and our Privacy Policy with you.
- Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens in your account. Tell us straight away at [contact email] if you think someone else has used it.
- Your club can remove your access, and you can delete your account at any time in App Settings.

### A3. Being part of a youth football community

Boost Huddle is used by families and young players. When you use it you must not:

- post anything abusive, threatening, discriminatory, sexual, or unsuitable for children;
- bully, harass or pressure anyone, including in Man of the Match votes or comments;
- share photos, videos or personal details of other people, especially children, without the permission the club requires;
- contact a child through Boost Huddle in a way that breaks your club's safeguarding policy;
- pretend to be someone else, or try to get into accounts, clubs or parts of the service you are not allowed into;
- upload anything that breaks the law or someone else's rights, or that contains viruses or harmful code;
- try to copy, scrape, overload or interfere with the service.

**Safeguarding concerns:** if you are worried about a child's safety, contact your club's welfare officer straight away. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. You can also report content in Boost Huddle to your club and to us at [safety contact email]. We will remove content that breaks these rules and may suspend accounts. Where the law requires, or a child may be at risk, we will share information with the police or other authorities.

### A4. Content you add

You keep ownership of the photos, videos, posts and comments you add. You give us permission to store, copy, resize and display them within Boost Huddle, and on your club's public page where the club publishes them, so that we can provide the service. This permission ends when the content is deleted, except for copies in backups which roll over within 30 days.

You must have the right to share anything you add. Your club and we may remove content at any time.

### A5. The service

We work hard to keep Boost Huddle running and secure, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of errors. We may change or improve features, and we will give clubs reasonable notice of important changes that affect them.

### A6. Our responsibility to you

If you use Boost Huddle as a parent, player or supporter, nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer under UK law, or our responsibility for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for fraud.

Boost Huddle is provided to consumers free of charge by your club. We are not responsible for decisions your club makes (for example team selection or fees), or for losses that were not foreseeable when you started using the service.

### A7. Ending your use

You can stop using Boost Huddle and delete your account at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, telling you why unless the law or someone's safety prevents it.

### A8. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we will show a notice in the app and email club owners at least [30] days before the change takes effect.

### A9. Law
