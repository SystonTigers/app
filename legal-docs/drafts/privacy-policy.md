## 1. About this policy

Boost Huddle is an app and website that grassroots football clubs use to run their teams: fixtures and results, squads, availability, live match updates, news and social media posts, photos and match highlights, Man of the Match votes, signing on and club payments.

Clubs on Boost Huddle are either **junior teams** (players under 18) or **adult teams**. Some things work differently for each, and this policy says where. We have written it for parents, players and club volunteers. There are short versions for young players and for adult players in section 13.

## 2. Who is responsible for your information

Two different organisations are responsible for different information:

- **Your football club** decides what information about its players, parents and members goes into Boost Huddle and what it is used for. For that information the club is the **controller** and we are its **processor**: we store and handle it for the club, on its instructions, under a written agreement (our Data Processing Terms). If you have a question about the information your club holds about you or your child, please ask the club first; we will help the club answer it.
- **We (Boost Huddle)** are the **controller** for the information we need to run the service ourselves: club owner and admin accounts, subscriptions and billing, keeping the service secure, and messages you send to us.

## 3. What information is used

### Information your club adds

- **Players:** name, squad number, position, date of birth, photo (and, if the club makes one, a cut-out of the photo for graphics), the season(s) they play in, line-ups, match events (for example goals, assists, cards, substitutions, minutes played, Man of the Match awards), availability and training attendance.
- **Signing on (only if the club switches it on):** date of birth, address, school (junior teams only), up to three emergency contacts (name, relationship, phone number and email address), any medical notes and allergies the family or player chooses to give, photo and video choices and agreement to the club's code of conduct.
- **Club content:** fixtures, results, news posts, photos, match videos and highlight clips, events, league tables, tactics and Team Talk conversations.

**Medical notes and allergies** are health information, which the law treats as especially sensitive. They are optional, are only shown to the club's staff and the player's own family (or the adult player themselves), and are never shown publicly or posted anywhere. Clubs should only ask for what they need to look after players. We do not ask for ethnicity, religion or other special category information, and we ask clubs not to add it in notes or free-text fields.

### Information you give us when you use the app

- **Account:** your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form), your name, your phone number, the club you belong to and your role (for example parent, player, coach, supporter).
- **What you do in the app:** availability answers and notes for the coaches, photo and video choices, Man of the Match and Goal of the Month votes, event replies, predictions games, Team Talk comments, reports of content you are worried about, and posts.
- **Notifications:** if you allow notifications, a notification address for your phone or browser so we can send you match alerts and reminders.
- **Messages to us:** anything you send when you contact us.

### Information for clubs that pay for Boost Huddle

- The club owner's name and email, the club name, the plan chosen and the payment status.
- Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card details.

### Information collected automatically

- **Sign-in and settings stored on your device:** the app and website keep your sign-in, your chosen club and any match updates that are still waiting to send (when there's no signal) in your browser or phone storage. These are strictly necessary for the service. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.
- **"At the match" on match day:** if you allow it, the app checks your phone's location against the ground's on match day, on your phone, so you aren't sent alerts about a match you are watching. Only "at the match: yes or no" is sent to us; your location itself is never sent or stored. You can say yes or no yourself instead.
- **Security and error logs:** our hosting provider records technical details of requests (such as IP address, time and the page requested) to keep the service secure and working. [If crash reporting is switched on in the phone app: crash reports containing technical information about the device and the error.]

Some things happen only on your own device and are never uploaded: removing the background from a player photo to make a cut-out (until a coach chooses to save it), and cutting a highlights video from the club's own match recording.

We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to anyone.

## 5. Junior teams (players under 18)

Junior teams have extra protections, designed with the UK Information Commissioner's Children's Code in mind:

- **Clubs add children's details, not children.** Player records are created and managed by club staff. Parents link their own account to their child with a code from the club, and answer for them (availability, photo and video consent, signing on).
- **Accounts.** Children under 13 should not have their own account; a parent or carer uses their own account instead. Everyone who signs up in the app confirms they are 13 or over, or a parent or carer.
- **Photos and videos** of a child are only shown publicly or posted once a parent or carer has said yes, and the parent can change their answer at any time.
- **What is public.** Each club has a public web page. By default it shows players by first name and surname initial (for example "Alfie S."), with squad numbers, positions, match statistics, results and Man of the Match winners, and no photos. A club can choose to show full names and photos; it should only do so with parents' agreement, and photos still need each parent's yes. Upcoming matches, kick-off times and grounds are only shown to signed-in members the club has let in, and a club can leave them out of social media posts too. The public page never shows dates of birth, ages, addresses, medical notes, emergency contacts, email addresses or phone numbers. Birthday posts appear in the club app only and never show a child's age.
- **Voting** is limited to signed-in members of the club. Running vote counts are hidden until voting closes.
- **No profiling or advertising.** We do not profile children, show them advertising or use their information for anything other than running their club.
- **Parents' rights.** A parent or carer can exercise the rights in section 9 on behalf of their child. Young people who are old enough to understand their rights (generally from about 12 in the UK) can also exercise them themselves.

## 6. Adult teams

For adult teams (players 18 and over), players look after their own information:

- **Players answer for themselves.** A player links their own account to their player page with a code from the club, and answers availability, photo and video choices and signing on themselves. There are no parent accounts.
- **Photos and videos are used unless you say no.** Your club may use your photo and video in the app, on its club page, in social media posts, live streams and highlights. You can say no at any time in the app (Photo & Video Consent), or by telling your club; from then on your photo is not shown publicly and staff are warned before sharing video of you. Posts already published may stay where they are, but your club can remove its own posts if you ask.
- **What is public.** By default the club page and posts show players' full names, photos (unless a player has said no), squad numbers, positions, match statistics, results, fixtures, kick-off times and grounds. A club can choose to show less. The public page never shows dates of birth, ages, addresses, medical notes, emergency contacts, email addresses or phone numbers.

## 7. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

- **Your club:** club staff can see the information in their club. Other members see what the club chooses to show them (for example the squad, fixtures and news); medical notes are only shown to staff and the player's own family (or the adult player).
- **The public, and social media, when your club posts:** your club's page, and the club's own Facebook and Instagram if it connects them, show what is described in sections 5 and 6. Once something is posted on Facebook or Instagram, Meta Platforms also handles it under its own privacy policy. Live match video is streamed on the club's own YouTube channel, under Google's privacy policy.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of your information;
- ask us to correct it;
- ask us to delete it;
- object to, or ask us to restrict, how it is used (for adult players, this includes saying no to your photo and video being used);
- ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere;
- withdraw consent where it is relied on (for example a parent's yes to photos, or consent for medical notes).

**Deleting your account:** you can delete your account at any time in the app (App Settings, then Delete Account). This immediately removes your account, your sign-in details, your links to players, your votes, event replies, game entries, comments and posts. Records your club keeps about a player (for example the squad list and match statistics) are the club's; ask the club to remove them.

To use any of your rights, contact your club (for club information) or us at [contact email]. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113), though we would appreciate the chance to help first.

## 11. How information is protected

- All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords are stored as secure one-way hashes, and the club's Facebook, Instagram and YouTube sign-ins are stored encrypted.
- Each club's information is kept separate, and every request is checked against the signed-in person's club and role; only club staff can change club information.
- New members can't see the club's information until club staff let them in.
- Sign-in tokens can be revoked when you sign out.
- Access to the systems is limited to the people who run Boost Huddle.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell the affected clubs and, where required, the ICO and you.
