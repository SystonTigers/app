## 1. About this policy

Boost Huddle is an app and website that grassroots football clubs use to run their teams: fixtures and results, squads, availability, news, photos, Man of the Match votes and club payments.

This policy explains what personal information is used, who is responsible for it, and the rights you have. We have written it for parents and young people as well as club volunteers. There is a short version for young players in section 12.

## 2. Who is responsible for your information

Two different organisations are responsible for different information:

- **Your football club** decides what information about its players, parents and members goes into Boost Huddle and what it is used for. For that information the club is the **controller** and we are its **processor**: we store and handle it for the club, on its instructions, under a written agreement (our Data Processing Terms). If you have a question about the information your club holds about you or your child, please ask the club first; we will help the club answer it.
- **We (Boost Huddle)** are the **controller** for the information we need to run the service ourselves: club owner and admin accounts, subscriptions and billing, keeping the service secure, and messages you send to us.

## 3. What information is used

### Information your club adds

- **Players:** name, squad number, position, date of birth, photo, the season(s) they play in, match events (for example goals, assists, cards, Man of the Match awards), availability and attendance.
- **Emergency contacts for players:** up to three contacts, with name, relationship, phone number and email address.
- **Club content:** fixtures, results, news posts, photos, videos, events, league tables and messages.

We do not ask for medical, health, ethnicity, religion or other special category information, and we ask clubs not to add it (for example in notes or free-text fields).

### Information you give us when you use the app

- **Account:** your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form), your name and the club you belong to, and your role (for example parent, player, coach).
- **What you do in the app:** your Man of the Match votes, event replies, predictions games, comments and posts.
- **Messages to us:** anything you send when you contact us.

### Information for clubs that pay for Boost Huddle

- The club owner's name and email, the club name, the plan chosen and the payment status.
- Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card details.

### Information collected automatically

- **Sign-in and settings stored on your device:** the app and website keep your sign-in and your chosen club in your browser or phone storage so you stay signed in. These are strictly necessary for the service. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.
- **Security and error logs:** our hosting provider records technical details of requests (such as IP address, time and the page requested) to keep the service secure and working. [If crash reporting is switched on in the phone app: crash reports containing technical information about the device and the error.]

We do not collect your location, and we do not use your information for advertising or sell it to anyone.

## 4. Why information is used, and the lawful basis

| What | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Player, parent and member information added by a club | So the club can organise its team, keep in touch with families and keep young players safe | Decided by the club, usually the club's **legitimate interests** in running the team, or the membership agreement between the club and the family |
| Emergency contacts | So the club can reach a parent or carer if something happens | The club's legitimate interests (and, in an emergency, protecting someone's **vital interests**) |
| Photos and videos of players | Club news, gallery and highlights | Decided by the club; clubs are responsible for having any consent needed from parents before sharing images of children, especially publicly |

| Service emails (password resets, sign-in links, reminders your club sends) | So the service works | Contract / the club's legitimate interests |

## 5. Children

Many players are under 18. We have designed the service with the UK Information Commissioner's Children's Code in mind:

- **Clubs add children's details, not children.** Player records are created and managed by club staff.
- **Accounts.** Children under 13 should not have their own account; a parent or carer uses their own account instead. Everyone who signs up in the app confirms they are 13 or over, or a parent or carer.
- **What is public.** Each club has a public web page. By default it shows players by first name and surname initial (for example "Alfie S."), with squad numbers, positions, match statistics and Man of the Match winners, and no photos. A club can choose to show full names and photos; it should only do so with parents' agreement. The public page never shows dates of birth, emergency contacts, email addresses or phone numbers.
- **Man of the Match voting** is limited to signed-in members of the club. Running vote counts are hidden until voting closes.
- **No profiling or advertising.** We do not profile children, show them advertising or use their information for anything other than running their club.
- **Parents' rights.** A parent or carer can exercise the rights in section 8 on behalf of their child. Young people who are old enough to understand their rights (generally from about 12 in the UK) can also exercise them themselves.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of your information;
- ask us to correct it;
- ask us to delete it;
- object to, or ask us to restrict, how it is used;
- ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere;
- withdraw consent where we rely on it.

**Deleting your account:** you can delete your account at any time in the app (App Settings, then Delete Account). This immediately removes your account, your sign-in details, your links to players, your votes, event replies, game entries, comments and posts. Records your club keeps about a player (for example the squad list and match statistics) are the club's; ask the club to remove them.

To use any of your rights, contact your club (for club information) or us at [contact email]. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113), though we would appreciate the chance to help first.

## 9. How long information is kept

- **Your account:** until you delete it, or until your club removes you. [Proposed: accounts with no sign-in for 2 years are deleted after a warning email.]
- **Club information:** for as long as the club uses Boost Huddle. When a club closes its account, its information is deleted within [90] days, unless it asks us to export it first.
- **Billing records:** 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax law requires.
- **Backups and logs:** database backups roll over after up to 30 days; security logs are kept for [up to 30 days].

## 10. How information is protected

- All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords are stored as secure one-way hashes.
- Each club's information is kept separate, and every request is checked against the signed-in person's club and role; only club staff can change club information.
- Sign-in tokens can be revoked when you sign out.
- Access to the systems is limited to the people who run Boost Huddle.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell the affected clubs and, where required, the ICO and you.

## 12. The short version for young players

- Your club uses Boost Huddle to run your team.
- Your club puts in your name, shirt number, position, birthday, photo and stats, plus your parents' contact details in case of an emergency.
- Your birthday and your family's phone numbers and emails are never shown on the public club page.
- Your first name, the first letter of your surname and your stats might be shown on your club's page. Your club can only show your full name and photo if your parents agree. If you are not happy with anything shown, tell your parent or coach.
- We never sell your information or show you adverts.
- You or your parent can ask to see, fix or delete your information at any time.
