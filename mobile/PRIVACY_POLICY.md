# Privacy Policy for Field Drop

**Last Updated:** January 14, 2026  
**Effective Date:** January 14, 2026

## Introduction

Syston Tigers ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Field Drop mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

**Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.**

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We collect the following personal information that you provide directly:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number
- **Profile Information:** Player position, squad number, date of birth
- **Contact Information:** Emergency contact details (for youth players)
- **Payment Information:** Processed securely through Stripe (we do not store credit card details)

### Usage Data
We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Location Data:** With your permission, we collect location data for match check-ins and nearby game features
- **Usage Analytics:** App interactions, features used, time spent in the App
- **Crash Reports:** Technical information when the App crashes to improve stability

### Content You Create
- **Posts and Comments:** Text, photos, and videos you share
- **Messages:** Communications within team chat
- **Match Data:** Game statistics, availability responses, match events
- **Videos:** Match highlights you record or upload

### Information from Third Parties
- **Social Media:** If you connect social accounts, we may receive your profile information
- **Payment Processors:** Transaction confirmation from Stripe

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Provide and maintain the App:**
   - Create and manage your account
   - Process match availability and team selections
   - Send fixture reminders and team updates
   - Enable chat and social features

2. **Improve and personalize the App:**
   - Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
   - Customize content based on your team and preferences
   - Test new features and improvements

3. **Communicate with you:**
   - Send push notifications about matches and team news
   - Respond to your inquiries and requests
   - Send important service announcements

4. **Process payments:**
   - Handle membership fees and merchandise purchases
   - Process refunds when applicable

5. **Ensure safety and security:**
   - Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
   - Enforce our Terms of Service
   - Comply with legal obligations

6. **With your consent:**
   - Use AI to analyze match videos for coaching insights (only with explicit permission)
   - Share your data with third parties as you've authorized

## How We Share Your Information

We DO NOT sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

### Within Your Team
- Your profile, availability status, and match statistics are visible to other team members
- Your posts and comments are visible to your team and authorized fans

### Service Providers
We share data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the App:
- **Cloudflare:** Hosting and content delivery
- **Stripe:** Payment processing
- **Expo/React Native:** Mobile app infrastructure
- **Google Cloud/Apple:** Push notifications
- **Sentry:** Crash reporting (if enabled)

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or to:
- Comply with legal processes or government requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of Field Drop, our users, or others

### Business Transfers
If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Access and Updates
- You can view and edit your profile information in the App settings
- You can update your notification preferences at any time

### Account Deletion
- You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account
- Upon deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance (e.g., payment records)

### Location Data
- You can enable or disable location services in your device settings
- Disabling location will limit features like match check-ins

### Marketing Communications
- You can opt-out of promotional communications in Settings
- You cannot opt-out of essential service communications

### Data Portability
- You can request a copy of your data by emailing privacy@fielddrop.app

### Cookie Preferences (Web Version)
- You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings

## Children's Privacy

Field Drop is designed for youth sports teams, including users under 13. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

- **Parental Consent:** We require verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13
- **Limited Data Collection:** We only collect information necessary for the App's functionality
- **No Marketing to Children:** We do not market to or sell children's data
- **Parent Rights:** Parents can review, delete, or refuse further collection of their child's information

**Parents:** To exercise your rights or if you have questions about your child's data, email us at privacy@fielddrop.app.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
- **Secure Authentication:** Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
- **Access Controls:** Limited employee access to personal data
- **Regular Audits:** We conduct security assessments regularly

**However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.** We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

- **Active Accounts:** Data retained while account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** Most data deleted within 30 days
- **Legal Compliance:** Some data retained longer if required by law (e.g., financial records for 7 years)
- **Aggregated Data:** De-identified analytics data may be retained indefinitely

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure adequate protections through:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses:** For transfers outside your region
- **Privacy Shield (US-EU):** Where applicable
- **Data Processing Agreements:** With all service providers
